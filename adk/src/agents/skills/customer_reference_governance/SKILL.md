# Customer Reference & Customer Reference Library Governance Skill (`K3`)

## 1. `Proof_Points` (`0% IMMUTABLE`) Slide Canvas Rule

-   **`Proof_Points` slides are `0% IMMUTABLE` on the slide canvas:**
    -   **NEVER** rewrite, extrapolate, or swap the customer name, logo, quotes,
        or quantitative percentages on a reusable Customer Reference
        (`Proof_Points`) slide.
    -   All customer-specific contextualization ("Why this peer proof point
        matters for `<Target Customer>`") MUST be placed inside the slide's
        **Speaker Notes** (`speaker_notes`).

## 2. Strict Product Disambiguation Protocol (from Golden Gate `CustomerRef`)

-   **Gemini Enterprise (Target, formerly Agentspace) vs. Gemini for
    Workspace:**
    -   When the pitch focuses on **Gemini Enterprise**, **Agentic Workplace
        Transformation**, or **Scale AI Apps and Agents**, **ONLY** select
        references and metrics that cite **Gemini Enterprise**, **Agentspace**,
        **Google Agent Development Kit (ADK)**, **BigQuery**, or **Google Cloud
        AI**.
    -   **REJECT** references that only cite generic *Duet AI* or *Gemini for
        Google Workspace* unless the customer explicitly requested Google
        Workspace productivity licensing.

## 3. The 5 Value Pillars for Speaker Note Impact Framing

When summarizing a customer reference or Customer Reference Library case study in **Speaker
Notes** or executive summaries, structure the impact across the **5 Value
Pillars** (prioritizing verified quantitative metrics):

1.  **Financial Impact:** Cost reduction, TCO savings, revenue growth,
    ROAS/conversion lift.
2.  **Operational Velocity:** Cycle-time reduction, developer productivity,
    faster time-to-market.
3.  **System & Scale:** Throughput, latency reduction, uptime reliability,
    petabyte-scale unification.
4.  **User / Customer Experience:** NPS/CSAT improvement, self-service
    resolution rate, personalization accuracy.
5.  **Strategic Transformation:** New business models, enterprise-wide AI
    adoption, security posture elevation.
