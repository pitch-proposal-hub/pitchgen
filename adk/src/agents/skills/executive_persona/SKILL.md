# Executive Persona Sales Enablement Guidelines

Use this skill when tailoring presentation decks, Executive Briefings, or messaging for C-level executives and technical decision makers.

## Core Executive Rules
- **Business Outcome Alignment**: Translate technical capabilities into stakeholder-specific business outcomes (TCO, margin expansion, customer LTV, engineering velocity, risk reduction).
- **Product Agnosticism & Grounding Delegation**: Persona playbooks provide the *buyer lens, decision psychology, and KPI metrics*. Always resolve active Google Cloud product names and technical capabilities dynamically from `product_knowledge` (`@product-knowledge`) and verified grounding context.
- **Altitude & Tone Calibration**: Match executive altitude. Focus on strategic business model evolution, competitive advantage, and risk mitigation. Avoid tactical DevOps/config minutiae for business executives.
- Check the relevant sub-reference file in `references/` for persona-specific decision drivers, KPI tables, and objection handling.
