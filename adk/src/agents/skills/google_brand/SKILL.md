# Google Brand Guidelines

Use these guidelines when generating or transforming slides to adhere to the official Google style.

## Core Colors

When a Google-branded slide is requested, use these exact hex codes:

-   **Google Blue**: `#4285F4`
-   **Google Red**: `#EA4335`
-   **Google Yellow**: `#FBBC05`
-   **Google Green**: `#34A853`

## Typography

-   **Headings / Title text**: Use **Product Sans** (or a clean, geometric sans-serif fallback if not available).
-   **Body text / Descriptions**: Use **Roboto** (or a clean, highly legible sans-serif font).

## Layout & Backgrounds

-   **Backgrounds**: Prefer clean white (`#FFFFFF`) or very light gray (`#F8F9FA`) backgrounds to ensure high contrast and professional readability.
-   **Margins & Grids**: Use generous padding and clear alignment. Do not crowd elements or overlap text.
-   **Aesthetic**: Follow a minimalist, flat design philosophy. Avoid heavy shadows, gradients (unless explicitly specified), or skeumorphic elements.
