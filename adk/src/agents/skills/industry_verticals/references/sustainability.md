# Enterprise GTM Playbook: Sustainability & Climate Tech

## 1. Industry Executive Summary & Macro Dynamics
Enterprise sustainability and climate tech organizations face strict global disclosure mandates (CSRD, SEC Climate Rules, ISSB), pressure for net-zero carbon pledges, and the challenge of auditing Scope 1, 2, and 3 emissions across global supply chains. Executive priorities center on **carbon accounting automation**, **deforestation and biodiversity satellite tracking**, **climate resilience modeling**, and **green cloud computing**.

## 2. Google Cloud Competitive Differentiation
- **Planetary Geospatial Leader**: **Google Earth Engine** hosts over 80 petabytes of satellite imagery and geospatial datasets, enabling real-time deforestation, flood risk, and agricultural yield monitoring that no competitor can match.
- **24/7 Carbon-Free Energy (CFE) Pioneer**: Google Cloud operates the cleanest global cloud infrastructure, matching annual electricity consumption 100% with renewable energy.
- **Built-In Carbon Footprint Accounting**: **Google Cloud Carbon Footprint** provides free, auditable, ISO-compliant greenhouse gas emissions reporting across all Google Cloud services.
- **AI for Climate Resilience**: **Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform** and **GraphCast** AI weather forecasting predict extreme weather events faster and more accurately than traditional physics-based models.

## 3. Executive Persona KPI Alignment (Cross-Skill: `@executive-persona`)
- **Chief Sustainability Officer (CSO) / CDO (`@cdo-persona`)**:
  - *Primary KPIs*: CSRD/SEC audit compliance, Scope 3 supplier data completeness, carbon reduction velocity.
  - *Value Proposition*: Unifying ESG metrics in **BigQuery** and generating regulatory disclosure reports via **Gemini Enterprise**.
- **CFO (`@cfo-persona`)**:
  - *Primary KPIs*: Carbon tax exposure, ESG rating impact on borrowing cost, climate risk value-at-risk.
  - *Value Proposition*: Quantifying climate flood/wildfire financial exposure on physical real estate portfolios.
- **CTO (`@cto-persona`)**:
  - *Primary KPIs*: IT data center carbon footprint, energy-efficient compute selection.
  - *Value Proposition*: Selecting low-carbon Google Cloud regions automatically via **Cloud Run** and **GKE**.

## 4. Segment-Targeting Playbook (Cross-Skill: `@segment-targeting`)
- **Enterprise Accounts (Global 2000 Corporate ESG Teams)**:
  - *Strategy*: Pitch enterprise-wide ESG data lakehouses on BigQuery, global Earth Engine licenses, and joint sustainability workshops (`https://your-sales-portal.example.com/catalog`).
- **Select Accounts (Mid-Market Enterprises, Supply Chain Leaders)**:
  - *Strategy*: Lead with turnkey carbon accounting: drop-in BigQuery ESG schemas.
- **Digital Natives (ClimateTech Startups, Carbon Credit Marketplaces)**:
  - *Strategy*: Emphasize Earth Engine APIs and scalable geospatial computing on GKE.

## 5. Enterprise Reference Architecture & High-Impact Use Cases
1. **Automated CSRD & SEC ESG Disclosure Report Copilot**:
   - *Architecture*: Multi-tier supplier invoices + energy meter feeds -> **BigQuery ESG Schema** -> **Gemini Enterprise** drafting audit-ready sustainability disclosures.
2. **Deforestation-Free Supply Chain Verification**:
   - *Architecture*: Supplier farm coordinates -> **Google Earth Engine** satellite time-series analysis -> verifying zero deforestation for EU Deforestation Regulation (EUDR) compliance.

## 6. Objection Handling & Risk Mitigation
- **Objection**: *"Our sustainability auditors require third-party verification of our cloud emissions calculations."*
  - **Google Cloud Response**: Show that **Google Cloud Carbon Footprint** methodologies are reviewed and verified by third-party auditors to conform to the **Greenhouse Gas (GHG) Protocol** and ISO 14064-1 standards.
