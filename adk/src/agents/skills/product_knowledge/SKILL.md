# Product Knowledge Skill: Google Cloud & Google AI / Gemini

This skill establishes the **Developer Knowledge API** as the dynamic,
authoritative single source of truth for all **Google Cloud products, solutions,
architectures, and Google AI capabilities**, and enforces the **October 2026
Gemini Brand Unification (`Enterprise-Brand-Unification`)**.

---

## 1. Core Mission & Scope

Use this skill to ensure presentations reflect **authoritative, real-time Google Cloud and Google AI capabilities**:

* **GCP Product Grounding**: Verify product capabilities, service descriptions, quotas, and architectural positioning against official documentation.
* **AI & Foundation Models**: Discover and accurately position currently active foundation models, multimodal tools, code companions, and agent runtimes.

*   **Dynamic Product Modernization & Brand Unification**: Autonomously migrate

## 3. October 2026 Gemini Brand Unification Directive (`Enterprise-Brand-Unification`)

### A. The Directive in 30 Seconds (The Chrome Enterprise Construct)

1.  **One Gemini Masterbrand for End Users**:
    *   Employees at work interact directly with **`Gemini`**, just as Chrome
        Enterprise users simply open Chrome.
    *   To the end user, the app and the agent are simply **`Gemini`**.
    *   We keep **`Gemini Enterprise`** strictly for **commercial SKUs and
        editions** (such as **`Gemini Enterprise Business edition`**, **`Gemini
        Enterprise Standard edition`**, and **`Gemini Enterprise Plus
        edition`**) and use **`Gemini for your business`** (or **`Gemini for
        business`**) when descriptively differentiating from the consumer
        offering in marketing without naming a SKU.
2.  **Shared Experience Across Surfaces**:
    *   Consumer and enterprise product teams share a single UI design language
        and mobile binary. Users get one consistent **`Gemini`** experience
        across web, desktop, mobile, Google Workspace, and Slack.
3.  **Clean Expressions in Execution**:
    *   Platform and developer capabilities use **`Gemini platform`** (lowercase
        **`"p"`**), such as **`Model Garden in Gemini platform`**, **`agent
        runtime in Gemini platform`**, and **`Antigravity in Gemini platform`**.
    *   Industry and business function solutions use **`Gemini for [industry]`**
        in **lowercase** (such as **`Gemini for legal`**, **`Gemini for
        financial services`**, and **`Gemini for customer experience`**).
4.  **Scope**:
    *   This directive applies across **all slide titles, subtitles, card
        headers, body copy, architecture diagrams, and speaker notes**.

### B. Strictly Prohibited Terms (Do Not Use Anywhere)

*   ⛔ **AVOID `Gemini Enterprise app` / `the GE app` / `the app tier` / `the
    app` (as a tier)**
*   ⛔ **AVOID `Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform` / `GEAP` / `Agent Platform` /
    `Gemini Enterprise platform` / `Gemini Platform` (capital P)**
*   ⛔ **AVOID `Sobi` / `Spark` / `Gemini agent` / `"Google Cloud's Gemini
    agent"`**
*   ⛔ **AVOID `Gemini Enterprise for Legal / Financial Services / CX / GECX`**
    *(use `"Gemini for [industry]"` in lowercase instead)*
*   ⛔ **AVOID Capitalized audience sub-brands** (such as `"Gemini Enterprise for
    Developers"`) *(use `"Gemini for your business"` or `"Gemini for
    developers"` in lowercase instead)*
*   ⛔ **AVOID Standalone edition names without prefix** (`"Business edition"`,
    `"Standard edition"`, `"Plus edition"` without `"Gemini Enterprise"` prefix)
*   ⛔ **AVOID `Vertex` / `Vertex AI`** *(when referring to Gemini platform
    capabilities)*
*   ⛔ **AVOID `Powered by Gemini`** *(use **`Built with Gemini`** instead)*

### E. How to Write for Common Scenarios

1.  **Writing for end users (everyday workflows)**:
    *   Call the app and the agent simply **`Gemini`** and name the surface
        where people work.
    *   *Reference example*: *"Ask Gemini in Google Docs to summarize project
        notes. Delegate follow-ups to Gemini in the AI Inbox, or collaborate
        with Gemini in Slack."*
2.  **Writing marketing copy that differentiates from consumer**:
    *   Use **`Gemini for your business`** when you are not referring to a
        specific commercial SKU.
    *   *Reference example*: *"Put Gemini to work for your business with
        built-in data protections and centralized admin controls."*
3.  **Writing a launch headline across surfaces**:
    *   Use **`Gemini users`** and **`Gemini platform`**.
    *   *Reference example*: *"Starting today, Gemini users can access Gemini
        3.8 Flash on web and mobile. Developers can also build with it using
        Model Garden in Gemini platform."*
4.  **Writing for developers**:
    *   Use **`Gemini platform`** (lowercase `"p"`).
    *   *Reference example*: *"Developers can build and govern agents in
        Antigravity, in their existing IDE, or via API in Gemini platform. They
        get ADK, MCP endpoints, A2A, and agent runtime in Gemini platform."*

## 4. Autonomous Modernization & Forward-Upgrade Protocol

When synthesizing or transforming presentation decks, apply live Developer
Knowledge findings and the canonical October 2026 Google Cloud GTM product
taxonomy:

### A. The Forward-Modernization Rule

* When a slide references a Google Cloud product or platform capability, verify its current official name via the Developer Knowledge API and canonical GTM guidelines.
*   Upgrade legacy or retired product names to the active official designation:
    *   **End-User App & Agent**: Modernize `"Gemini Enterprise app"`, `"the GE
        app"`, `"Sobi"`, `"Spark"`, `"Google Cloud's Gemini agent"`, `"Duet
        AI"`, and `"Agentspace"` to **`Gemini`** (or **`Gemini for your
        business`** when differentiating from consumer in marketing).
    *   **Developer & Agent Platform**: Modernize `"Vertex AI"` (when referring
        to Gemini platform capabilities), `"Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform"`,
        `"GEAP"`, `"Agent Platform"`, `"Gemini Enterprise platform"`, and
        `"Gemini Platform"` (capital P) to **`Gemini platform`** (lowercase
        `"p"`), or specific capabilities such as **`Model Garden in Gemini
        platform`**, **`agent runtime in Gemini platform`**, **`Antigravity in
        Gemini platform`**, **`Agent Garden`**, or **`Agent Registry`**.
    *   **Industry & Function Solutions**: Modernize `"Gemini Enterprise for
        Legal"`, `"Gemini Enterprise for Financial Services"`, and `"GECX"` /
        `"Gemini Enterprise for Customer Experience"` to lowercase **`Gemini for
        legal`**, **`Gemini for financial services`**, and **`Gemini for
        customer experience`**.
    *   **Data Catalog & Metadata Governance**: Modernize legacy `"Dataplex"` or
        `"Dataplex Catalog"` to **`Knowledge Catalog`**.
    *   **AI Supercomputing & Systems**: Modernize legacy `"AI Supercompute"` or
        generic `"Compute Layer"` to **`AI Hypercomputer`** (co-designed
        architecture across TPU v5p/v6e and GPU clusters).
    *   **Attribution Phrasing**: Modernize `"Powered by Gemini"` to **`Built
        with Gemini`**.
    *   **Foundation Models & Generative Media**: Verify active production
        models dynamically via the Developer Knowledge API
        (`DeveloperKnowledgeWorkflow`). Never output deprecated model versions
        or statically assume version numbers.

### B. Strict Anti-Downgrade Rule

*   **Never Downgrade**: If slide input already uses contemporary, verified
    product names (e.g., `Gemini`, `Gemini platform`, `Model Garden in Gemini
    platform`, `Gemini for financial services`, `Gemini Enterprise Standard
    edition`, `AI Hypercomputer`, `Knowledge Catalog`), never replace or
    downgrade them to older or retired aliases (e.g., `"Vertex AI"`, `"Gemini
    Enterprise Agent Platform"`, `"GEAP"`, `"Gemini Enterprise app"`, `"Sobi"`,
    `"Spark"`, `"GECX"`, `"AI Supercompute"`, `"Dataplex"`).
*   Maintain nomenclature consistency across all slides and speaker notes in the
    deck.

### C. Zero Hallucination & Exact Nomenclature Integrity

*   **No Fictional Portmanteaus**: Never invent synthetic product names,
    imaginary platform layers, or composite acronyms (e.g., `"Gemini platform
    Model Garden"`, `"GEAP Model Garden"`, `"Token Factory Layer"`, `"ClickHouse
    BigQuery"`, `"Looker Telemetry Hub"`, `"Vertex AI Garden"`).
*   **Official Verification**: Every Google Cloud product name and architectural
    capability in the output must be grounded in verified documentation
    retrieved via Developer Knowledge and official GTM taxonomy.

## 5. Architecture Blueprint & Technology Stack Preservation

When transforming architecture diagrams, stack hierarchy cards, or technology blueprints:

1.  **Unified Gemini Experience & Platform Capabilities**:
    *   Frame the experience across surfaces (`Gemini on web, desktop, and
        mobile, and via API in Gemini platform`) and pair developer capabilities
        cleanly with **`in Gemini platform`** (`Model Garden in Gemini
        platform`, `agent runtime in Gemini platform`, `Antigravity in Gemini
        platform`) — never as a stack diagram with `"the GE app"` on top of
        `"GEAP"` or separate sub-brands a customer has to assemble.
    *   Frame governance as an intrinsic platform property: **`Agents that
        cannot exist without an identity`**.
2.  **Platform Components & Tiers Are Immutable**:
    *   Official product names, architectural framework tiers (e.g. experiences,
        intelligent orchestration, data lakehouse, infrastructure), and
        multi-cloud platform labels (*Google Cloud Storage*, *AWS*, *Azure*,
        *On-Prem*) define exact technical boundaries.
    *   Preserve verified product labels and structural boxes intact (while
        upgrading retired terms like `Vertex AI`, `GEAP`, or `Gemini Enterprise
        Agent Platform` to `Gemini platform`) without inserting speculative or
        made-up terminology.
3.  **Domain Customization Boundary**:
    *   Focus narrative tailoring on **problem statements, operational
        workflows, customer pain points, use cases, and business outcomes**.
    *   Keep verified technical stack labels and system architecture diagrams
        technically pristine.
