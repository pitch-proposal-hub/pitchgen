# Agentic Data Cloud [Primary]

## What's the pitch for a customer/prospect? Why change? Why Google?

Activate your entire data estate on a unified, intelligent, self-managing data platform. Connect operational databases, real-time analytics, and AI models into an active data fabric that powers autonomous agentic experiences and intelligent business automation.

## Target Persona

- Chief Data Officer (CDO) / Chief Analytics Officer (CAO)
- VP of Data Engineering / Head of Business Intelligence
- Chief Information Officer (CIO)

## Core Capabilities & Offerings

- **BigQuery + Gemini**: Serverless multimodal data warehouse with native SQL AI functions, real-time analytics, and data governance.
- **AlloyDB & Spanner**: High-performance, globally scalable transactional databases built for real-time vector search and AI agent operational memory.
- **Dataplex**: Autonomous data cataloging, lineage tracking, and governance for enterprise AI grounding.
