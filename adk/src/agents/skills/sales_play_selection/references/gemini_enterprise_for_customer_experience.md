# Gemini Enterprise for Customer Experience [Growth]

## What's the pitch for a customer/prospect? Why change? Why Google?

Transform customer journeys from fragmented touchpoints into seamless, agentic experiences across search, commerce, and customer service. Google's conversational AI and multimodal reasoning turn traditional chatbots into empathetic, multi-turn AI agents capable of resolving inquiries, recommending products, and completing end-to-end transactions.

## Target Customer

B2C and B2B enterprises with high customer engagement volumes: Retail & E-commerce, Financial Services, Telecommunications, Travel & Hospitality, and Healthcare.

## Target Persona

- Chief Marketing Officer (CMO)
- Chief Customer Officer (CCO) / Head of Customer Care
- VP of Contact Center / Customer Experience (CX)
- Chief Digital Officer (CDO)

## Problem Statement

Customers face frustrating, disconnected service interactions with legacy IVR and rigid rule-based bots that fail on complex queries. Contact centers experience high turnover and escalating support costs while missing revenue conversion opportunities due to poor personalization.

## Offering

- **Customer Service Agents**: Multimodal conversational agents powering voice and digital channels with real-time sentiment analysis and CRM integration.
- **Agent Assist**: Real-time suggestion engine providing human agents with live summarization, knowledge base retrieval, and recommended next steps.
- **Conversational Commerce & Search**: Vertex AI Search for Retail and commerce agents that understand natural language queries and intent.

## Value to Customers

- **Deflection & First-Contact Resolution**: 40–60%+ automation of routine tier-1 inquiries.
- **Lower Handle Times**: 30–45% reduction in average handling time (AHT) with live agent assist.
- **Revenue Uplift**: 15–25% increase in conversion through guided conversational shopping.
