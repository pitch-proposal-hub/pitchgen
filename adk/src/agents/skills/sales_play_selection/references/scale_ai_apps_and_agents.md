# Scale AI Apps and Agents [Primary]

## What's the pitch for a customer/prospect? Why change? Why Google?

Achieve Agentic AI Transformation on a unified AI platform. Build, deploy and
manage the full spectrum of AI applications and agents using the unified
platform offered by Google Cloud. Seamlessly move AI projects from pilot to
production, whether customers are just starting their AI journey or looking to
consolidate their various AI initiatives onto a single, strategic platform.

-   CTO / CIO
-   CPO / VP Product
-   VP Platform Engineering/Head of MLOps

## Problem Statement

The market has hit an inflection point where the 'wild west' of experimentation
is over, but customers are now facing the "AI Production Challenge." This is
made up of three self-reinforcing problems: they are stuck in "Pilot Purgatory,"
struggling with reliability and scale; they are paying a crippling "Integration
Tax" to stitch together fragmented tools; and they face a paralyzing "Trust Gap"
fueled by Shadow AI. This complexity is only increasing as every business
building on AI begins to view their work through the lens of proactive,
"agentic" systems.

## Offering

Our answer is Gemini Enterprise, a single, AI-optimized product designed to move
customers from pilot to production with confidence. Gemini Enterprise provides
two core elements: first, Model Choice & Flexibility through the Model Garden,
offering strategic access to Gemini, 3rd Party, and Open-Source models. Second,
a complete set of Enterprise-Ready Capabilities across four pillars (clients,
agent, tools, governance), including surfaces to Build & Deploy agents (from
Antigravity and Agent Studio to Agent Engine) and built-in governance (where
agents cannot exist without an identity, grounded with enterprise connectors and
security).

## Solutions/Use Cases/Workloads

-   **Agentic AI**: Architect bespoke, mission-critical agents that plan,
    reason, and deeply integrate with your proprietary systems.
-   **Model Garden**: Access best-in-class models via a single, serverless API
    with the industry's best price-performance and zero lock-in.
-   **Platform Capabilities**: Eliminate 'pilot purgatory' with a single pane of
    glass that manages security, cost, & compliance from prototype to
    production.

## Value to Customers

-   **Innovate Faster**: Move from concept to production in weeks, not months,
    by using a single, AI-optimized platform that eliminates the "complexity
    tax" of stitching together disconnected tools
-   **Drive Growth**: Build new, revenue-generating applications and agents by
    combining multimodal understanding of complex data, AI-driven
    personalization and automated execution of real-world business actions
-   **Operate Smarter**: Automate complex workflows with AI you can trust,
    leveraging a platform with built-in enterprise-grade governance, security,
    and reliability to slash operational cost and risk
