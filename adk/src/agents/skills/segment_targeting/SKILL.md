# Segment Targeting

## Overview

This skill helps the agent tailor sales approaches, messaging, and
recommendations based on the customer's segment.

## Segment Guidelines

### SMB (Small and Medium Business)

-   **Focus**: Ease of use, immediate value, cost-effectiveness.
-   **Gotchas**: Avoid overly complex solutions or long implementation plans.

### Startups

-   **Focus**: Scalability, innovation, speed to market, flexible pricing.
-   **Gotchas**: They may pivot quickly; ensure solutions can adapt.

### Corporate

-   **Focus**: Efficiency, reliability, integration with existing systems.
-   **Gotchas**: Balance need for scale with budget constraints.

### Enterprise

-   **Focus**: Security, compliance, scalability, custom solutions, ROI.
-   **Gotchas**: Be prepared for long evaluation processes and strict security
    requirements.

### Strategic

-   **Focus**: Digital transformation, co-innovation, executive alignment.
-   **Gotchas**: Requires deep understanding of their business and industry.
