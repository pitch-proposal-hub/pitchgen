# Business Value Copywriting & Compliance Guidelines

Use these rules when writing copy or structuring slide bullets for business
value narratives, strategic pitches, and C-level executive presentations.

## 1. Google Brand Voice & Executive Headline Framing (`sites.google.com/google.com/overview/brand-in-action/voice-and-tone`)

*   **Target C-Suite:** Use confident, conversational, forward-looking language
    focused on facts and strategic value (*"Get to the point"*, *"Write like you
    talk"*).
*   **Avoid `-ing` Gerund Openers & Imperative Slogans:**
    *   NEVER start slide headlines or outline items with continuous/gerund
        `-ing` verbs (*"Reimagining..."*, *"Elevating..."*, *"Uniting..."*,
        *"Activating..."*, *"Scaling..."*, *"Orchestrating..."*,
        *"Establishing..."*, *"Unlocking..."*). Leading `-ing` fragments bury
        the subject, sound robotic/automated, and create monotonous rhythm
        across a deck.
    *   Do NOT write scolding or bare imperative command titles like *"Unlock
        Agentic Commerce"*, *"Automate Employee Workflows"*, or *"Stop Copying
        Data. Start Closing Deals."*
*   **Preferred Strategic Headline Framing (Active Voice & Crisp Contrast):**
    *   *Subject + Finite Active Verb + Outcome:* "Autonomous agents transform
        global banking workflows" / "One governed data fabric unifies 169PB on
        BigQuery"
    *   *From Current State to Future State:* "From fragmented copilots to an
        autonomous enterprise"
    *   *Capability Meets Customer Scale:* "Alphabet AI research meets HSBC
        global banking scale"
    *   *Crisp Noun Phrase:* "Joint governance and a phased path to production"
*   **Ban Subjective Hype Words:** Do NOT use hype words, empty adjectives, or buzzwords that offer no concrete proof.
    *   *Banned words:* "game-changing", "revolutionary", "groundbreaking",
        "unleash", "leverage", "streamline", "cutting-edge".
    *   *Alternative:* Instead of "unleash the power of AI to streamline
        operations," write: "AI agents automate core operations."

## 2. Outcome-First Bullet Structure

*   Always format bullets by putting the **business outcome/metric first**,
    followed by the **enabling mechanism/solution**.
*   *Good:* "Reduce cost-to-serve by 20–30% by automating backend claims using
    BigQuery and Document AI."
*   *Bad:* "Use BigQuery and Document AI to automate backend claims and reduce
    costs by 20–30%."

## 3. Strict Compliance Guardrails (No Guarantees)

*   Do NOT promise or guarantee specific business results for the customer.
*   Use helper verbs that express capability rather than certainty: **"can,"
    "helps," "enables," "unlocks," "supports."**
*   *Good:* "Helps reduce customer churn by up to 15%..."
*   *Bad:* "Will reduce customer churn by 15%..."
*   Frame case studies as *past results* achieved by other clients, not future
    guarantees for the target company.

## 4. Consistently Formatted Metrics

*   Always use whole integers (round half up) for metrics unless the value is
    below 0.5.
*   Prefer presenting ranges (e.g., "15–20%") where exact numbers vary to remain
    conservative.
*   Always explicitly name the KPI being measured.
