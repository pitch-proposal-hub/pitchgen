# WIN Cycle

This skill provides guidance on Google Cloud's WIN (What's Important Now) Cycle
sales process.

## WIN Cycle Principles

-   Standardized sales process across Google Cloud.
-   Applicable at an opportunity level.
-   Continuously evolving.

## Sales Stages

1.  **Planning & Lead Gen**: Understand the business, ensure customer outcome
    focused.
2.  **Qualify**: Identify needs aligning on the problem to solve.
3.  **Position & Build Business Case**: Explore solutions, clarity on conceptual
    solution and ROI.
4.  **Technical Solutioning**: Solution business & technical requirements,
    validated design.
5.  **Propose & Negotiate**: Confirm path forward, demonstrate technical &
    business value.
6.  **Mobilize & Deliver**: Plan & move to production, drive success from day
    one.
7.  **Optimize & Grow**: Drive adoption delivering ongoing value realization.

## Roles

-   **FSR (Field Sales Representative)**: Central orchestrator of “Team Google”
    resources. Leads in Stages 0 and 1.
-   **Platform CE (Platform Customer Engineer)**: Lead technical authority for
    platform sales. Default technical partner for FSRs. Primarily involved in
    S2-S4.
-   **Practice CE (Practice Customer Engineer)**: Partner with Platform CE in
    specialization area.
-   **Outcome CE (Outcome Customer Engineer)**: Drive initial and ongoing
    workloads & revenue ramp.
-   **GCC (Google Cloud Consulting)**: Services strategy, technical consulting.
-   **CLL (Customer Learning Lead)**: Learning Subject Matter Expert.
